Haven is a Matrix client for Android, the companion to Haven Desktop. It is based on Element X and brings in the features of SchildiChat Next.

Highlights:
- Bottom spaces bar with swipe navigation, including nested spaces
- Configurable chat list sorting: unread on top, pinned favourites, low priority at the bottom
- Unread counts, bridge icons and URL previews
- Richer message rendering: spoilers, collapsible details, tables and inline custom emotes
- Customisable message bubbles, freeform reactions and many more tweaks in the Turtle Tweaks settings

The Matrix protocol is a decentralized approach to modern messaging, allowing you to select a server provider of your choice (or even self-host your own server), enabling you to communicate with end-to-end encryption, shared message history across multiple devices, and more.

More information about Haven: https://haven.software
More information about the Matrix protocol: https://matrix.org/
